About Lily of the Valley Ministry

LOTV Ministry

The Mission

Lily of the Valley Ministry coordinates social services delivery through a network of local chapters connected to a central National HQ. The organization connects families in need with qualified volunteers, engages donors to fund meaningful services, and empowers parish communities to serve their neighbors — all through a unified, technology-driven approach.

Phase 3 — New Content Creation
