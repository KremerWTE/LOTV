Our Mission

Making Christ’s Love Visible

We are a lay Catholic ministry founded on the belief that no family should walk through the sorrow of infertility, a difficult pregnancy, or baby loss without the love of Christ made tangible. We minister to people of all faith backgrounds.

How We Serve

The Prayer Care Package

Our primary ministry is mailing personalized Prayer Care Packages to families navigating difficult early-life circumstances. Each package is unique — assembled based on the family’s specific situation, whether infertility, prenatal diagnosis, miscarriage, or infant loss.

Items vary based on donations and inventory, but our goal is always the same: to encourage families to maintain hope, pray during difficult times, and feel supported by God’s love through the hands of our community.

For families experiencing loss after 20 weeks or infant loss: In addition to the care package, we provide periodic grief support materials — including a series of four short books distributed throughout the first year following loss. These can be read all at once or in small portions whenever you are ready.

Monthly Prayer Night

We Pray Together
on the 28th

On the 28th of every month — the feast day of St. Gianna Molla — our entire community gathers in prayer for every family who has reached out to LOTV. Whether you are navigating infertility, a prenatal diagnosis, or the loss of a baby, your intention is prayed over each month.

We believe in the Communion of Saints and the power of intercessory prayer — including the prayers of the babies in heaven who intercede for their families from above.

Our Patroness

St. Gianna Beretta Molla

Lily of the Valley Ministry is also known as St. Gianna Molla Catholic Ministry in honor of our heavenly patroness — a physician, wife, and mother who gave her life to protect the life of her unborn daughter Gianna Emanuela.

St. Gianna’s witness speaks directly to every family we serve: that life is sacred from conception, that suffering united to Christ bears fruit, and that love is never wasted. She is our model and our intercessor.

“Love and sacrifice are as intimately connected as the sun and light.”

— St. Gianna Beretta Molla

We gather to pray on the 28th of each month in honor of her feast day (April 28), interceding for all families who have entrusted their intentions to LOTV. We believe she prays with us.
