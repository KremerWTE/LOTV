Common Questions

We’re Happy to Answer

Everything you need to know about how LOTV works, who we serve, and how your gift is used.

Who can request a prayer care package or services? ▼
Any family navigating infertility, a prenatal diagnosis, postnatal medical concerns, or pregnancy and infant loss may request support — regardless of where they are in their faith journey. LOTV is a Catholic apostolate but serves all families who need care. There is no cost to families for any LOTV service.
How are funds from donations used? ▼
100% of all donations go directly to family support services: prayer care package materials and delivery, volunteer coordination, grief counseling referrals, memorial support, and parish ministry training. LOTV has no paid executive staff — our leadership is volunteer-driven, keeping overhead extremely low.
Is my donation tax-deductible? ▼
LOTV Private Foundation’s 501(c)(3) application is currently pending with the IRS. Once approved, all gifts made after our founding date will be retroactively tax-deductible. We will notify all donors immediately upon IRS confirmation. Receipts are issued for all donations.
Is LOTV only for Catholic families? ▼
No. While LOTV is a Catholic apostolate rooted in Catholic faith and the intercession of St. Gianna Molla, our care is extended to any family in need. We serve families of all faith backgrounds with compassion and dignity, and families are never required to participate in any faith-based activities to receive support.
How do I refer a family who needs help? ▼
Referrals can be made by anyone — a family member, friend, pastor, nurse, or doctor. Visit our care package request page or call (555) 000-0001. For urgent situations, our diocese coordinators can be reached directly through your parish team.
How can my parish get involved or become a certified partner? ▼
Parishes can apply for LOTV partnership and certification through the Ministry Team Portal. Certified parish teams gain access to training, printable resources, referral forms, and direct support from LOTV diocese coordinators. Contact us at parish@lotvministry.org to begin.
How do I donate physical items from the wishlist? ▼
Items can be shipped directly from our Amazon wishlist to LOTV, or dropped off at any partner parish. Items are collected weekly by volunteers, assembled into prayer care packages, and delivered to families within 48–72 hours of a referral. Contact us for your nearest drop-off parish.

Our Patroness

St. Gianna Beretta Molla

Lily of the Valley Ministry is also known as St. Gianna Molla Catholic Ministry in honor of our heavenly patroness — a physician, wife, and mother who gave her life to protect the life of her unborn daughter. Canonized by Pope John Paul II in 2004.

St. Gianna’s witness speaks directly to every family we serve: that life is sacred, that suffering united to Christ bears fruit, and that love is never wasted. Our community gathers to pray on the 28th of each month in her honor.

“Love and sacrifice are as intimately connected as the sun and light.”

— St. Gianna Beretta Molla
☪ Visit the St. Gianna Relic — A relic is available for veneration at St. Raphael Catholic Church, Raleigh, NC during the 7:00 AM daily Mass. Contact info@LOTVministry.org for details.
LOTV Private Foundation

The LOTV Private Foundation

The Lily of the Valley Private Foundation is the charitable arm of LOTV Ministry, established to provide sustainable funding for family support services, parish training, and grief care programs across the country. The foundation ensures that every family receives care regardless of financial circumstance.
