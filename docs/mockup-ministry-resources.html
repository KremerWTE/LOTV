Frequently Asked Questions
How do I renew my certification?
Complete the required Level modules in the Training Library and submit via the My Certification tab. Your coordinator will confirm within 5 business days.
Can I add another team member to our parish?
Yes — share your parish access code or use Manage Team in My Certification. New members start with observer access until their first module is complete.
How do I refer a family to LOTV?
Use the Family Referral Form under Protocols & Forms. Urgent cases can be escalated directly to your diocese coordinator by phone.
I have a suggestion for a new resource.
We love hearing from parish teams. Use the message form above with category "Resource request or suggestion" — all recommendations go directly to our content team.
