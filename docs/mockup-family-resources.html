How It Works

Simple. Compassionate. Free to Families.

Every prayer care package is hand-assembled by LOTV volunteers and funded entirely through donations. There is no cost to the family — ever.

1

Choose a Prayer Care Package

Select the type of prayer care package that best fits what the family needs right now — comfort, meals, memory-keeping, or all three.

2

Tell Us About the Family

Share delivery details and an optional personal note. You can be the one ordering — the family does not need to do anything.

3

We Prepare & Deliver

Our team hand-assembles the package and coordinates delivery within 3–5 business days of your request.

4

We Follow Up

With the family's permission, our staff will reach out to see if they would like additional support from LOTV Ministry.

Prayer Care Package

What’s Inside

Every package is hand-assembled by LOTV volunteers, infused with prayer, and mailed free of charge to the family. A $50 donation covers the full cost of one package — but the family is never asked to pay.

✉
Handwritten Letter
A personal, handwritten letter from LOTV Ministry — composed with love and prayer by our team.
🍵
Lily Essential Oil & Tea
Carefully selected to touch the senses with peace and calm in the midst of sorrow.
🔖
Sacramentals & Prayer Cards
A rosary, St. Gianna prayer card, and other sacramentals to anchor the family in faith.
💐
Personalized Keepsake
A personalized bracelet with the initials of children — including those in heaven.
📚
Grief Book Series
For loss families, a selection from LOTV’s curated 4-book Catholic grief series.

A note about faith: We are a Catholic Ministry; however, we serve individuals of all faiths or no faith at all. Every family is welcome. Every package is given with the same love.
